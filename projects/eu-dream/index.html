Project context

Project Overview

EU-DREAM develops consumer-oriented digital energy services to support flexible electricity demand, energy management and participation in the energy transition. The European project connects household flexibility with retail pricing, consumer preferences and digital energy management across six European Living Labs.

My technical contribution

My Contribution

  • Develop optimization-based models of residential electricity demand and flexibility.
  • Represent flexible household technologies, particularly heat pumps and electric vehicle charging.
  • Evaluate the influence of dynamic electricity prices and capacity-based network charges on household scheduling decisions.
  • Examine flexibility under consumer preferences, comfort requirements and operational constraints.
