Project Overview

EU-DREAM is a Horizon Europe research and innovation project that aims to make digital energy services more accessible, effective and valuable for European consumers and energy communities.

The project brings together energy companies, research organizations and technology providers to develop next-generation digital solutions for energy management and consumer participation. Its activities cover data-driven energy services, household digital twins, artificial intelligence, flexibility and innovative market mechanisms, with demonstrations across six European Living Labs.

A central objective is to help consumers participate more actively in the energy transition while accounting for their preferences, practical constraints and potential economic benefits.

My technical contribution

My Work

At VITO / EnergyVille, I work on residential electricity demand and flexibility modelling, with a particular focus on the interaction between flexible household technologies and electricity pricing.

My work involves modelling the operation of heat pumps and electric vehicles, evaluating their scheduling flexibility under different consumer preferences and operating constraints, and examining how electricity tariffs and network charges influence household energy costs and demand patterns.

My technical contribution

Focus Areas

  • Residential demand flexibility: Modelling how households can adjust electricity consumption while respecting comfort and service requirements.
  • Flexible technologies: Representing heat pumps and electric vehicle charging as controllable household electricity demand.
  • Retail electricity pricing: Evaluating responses to dynamic electricity tariffs and time-dependent price signals.
  • Network tariff design: Studying how capacity-based and other network charging structures influence consumption and flexibility incentives.
  • Consumer heterogeneity: Considering differences in household preferences, automation and flexibility availability.
