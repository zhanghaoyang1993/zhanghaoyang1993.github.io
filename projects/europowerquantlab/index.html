Project Overview

EuroPowerQuantLab is my independent research and development project aimed at building a reproducible environment for quantitative analysis of European electricity markets.

The project is motivated by the increasing availability of historical electricity market data and the need to connect market understanding with systematic forecasting, strategy evaluation and risk-aware decision-making.

Its longer-term objective is to provide a transparent and extensible research workflow covering day-ahead, intraday and balancing/imbalance markets.

My technical contribution

My Work

I am developing the platform as a personal research environment, starting with historical data availability, data organization and the design of a backtesting framework.

The intention is to connect electricity market fundamentals, historical price behaviour, predictive modelling and trading strategy evaluation within a consistent and maintainable Python-based workflow.

Research Scope

  • Market data: Collecting and organizing historical electricity market data, with attention to market areas, time resolution, data availability and quality.
  • Market fundamentals: Exploring the relationships between electricity prices, generation, demand, renewable production and system conditions.
  • Forecasting research: Developing and evaluating quantitative approaches to electricity price and market outcome forecasting.
  • Strategy backtesting: Designing historical evaluation methods for electricity market bidding and trading strategies.
  • Risk and performance analysis: Establishing consistent metrics for strategy performance, transaction assumptions and risk evaluation.

Development Principles

The platform is designed around several principles: reproducibility, transparent modelling assumptions, modular data processing, maintainable code and a clear separation between historical information and information that would have been available at the time of a trading decision.

Methods & Tools
