Project Overview

EuroPowerQuantLab is my independent Python research project for European electricity market data analysis, forecasting research and historical strategy backtesting. The platform is under development, starting with historical data organization and a reproducible framework for evaluating bidding and trading strategies.

Planned capabilities

Development Scope

  • European day-ahead, intraday and balancing/imbalance market data.
  • Historical data processing and reproducible quantitative analysis.
  • Electricity price forecasting research.
  • Bidding and trading strategy backtesting.
  • Performance evaluation and risk analysis.

These are development objectives; implemented capabilities and reproducible findings will be documented progressively.
