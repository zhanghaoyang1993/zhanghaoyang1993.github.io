Research question

How do market rules and grid constraints affect the incentives of participants who learn to bid strategically?

My contribution

Developed research on local market structures and strategic participant behaviour, bringing together distributed optimization, reinforcement learning and network-constrained market clearing.

Approach

  1. Design hierarchical local market structures with multi-cut Benders decomposition.
  2. Simulate strategic bidding and assess the reliability of multi-agent learning outcomes.
  3. Study congestion management and pricing mechanisms for local flexibility markets.

Status & outputs

A PhD thesis and peer-reviewed publications document the modelling methods and simulation studies. See the linked publication catalogue for the original evidence.
