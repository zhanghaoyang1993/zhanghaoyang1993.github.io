Project context

Project Overview

MEGAMIND investigates data-driven and self-managed local electricity systems, linking distributed resources, grid operation and market arrangements. Within one PhD research track, I studied network-constrained local market design, strategic bidding and market power using optimization and multi-agent simulation.

My technical contribution

My Research

  • Designed hierarchical local electricity and flexibility market structures using mathematical optimization and decomposition methods.
  • Developed multi-agent simulation environments to investigate strategic bidding and participant incentives.
  • Applied reinforcement learning and multi-agent reinforcement learning to model adaptive bidding behaviour.
  • Investigated market power detection, pricing mechanisms and market-design alternatives.
  • Evaluated the reliability and behaviour of learning-based market simulation methods.

Related but independent visiting research at Empa addressed arbitrage across sequential local markets using hierarchical MARL.
