Research question

How can public market data help reconstruct historical clearing outcomes and examine changes in European market integration?

My contribution

Work on reconstructing the Core flow-based day-ahead electricity market using public JAO and NEMO data. The modelling scope includes historical validation, offshore bidding zones and market integration scenarios.

Approach

  1. Use public market and flow-based capacity data as inputs to historical reconstruction.
  2. Represent network constraints through CNEC, PTDF and RAM concepts in flow-based market coupling (FBMC).
  3. Compare reconstructed market outcomes with historical observations and examine alternative market integration scenarios.

Status & outputs

Ongoing modelling and historical validation. This overview describes the research scope; project results are not yet presented here.
