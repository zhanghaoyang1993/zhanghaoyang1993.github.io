Project Overview

CROCODILE investigates how offshore electricity infrastructure can be integrated efficiently into European electricity markets. As offshore wind generation expands and interconnected offshore energy hubs emerge, market design and cross-border coordination become increasingly important for both system operation and investment decisions.

The project examines alternative market arrangements for hybrid offshore grids, considering their implications for day-ahead electricity markets, balancing and reserve requirements, and long-term investment incentives. Its broader objective is to support efficient offshore wind integration while improving the allocation of benefits and risks among market participants.

My technical contribution

My Work

At VITO / EnergyVille, my work focuses on reconstructing historical market-clearing outcomes for the European Core flow-based day-ahead market using publicly available information. This involves developing a market-clearing model that approximates relevant aspects of the EUPHEMIA algorithm to support market-design analysis.

Project-wide objectives

Focus Areas

  • Offshore market integration: Alternative arrangements for integrating offshore generation and interconnected offshore grids into European electricity markets.
  • Cross-border market coordination: The interaction between bidding-zone design, transmission capacity allocation and market coupling.
  • Market efficiency: The implications of different market arrangements for electricity market outcomes and overall economic efficiency.
  • System and investment considerations: The relationship between market design, balancing requirements and long-term investment incentives.
