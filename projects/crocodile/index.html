Project Overview

CROCODILE examines how different offshore bidding-zone and market-integration designs affect the integration of offshore wind and the operation of European electricity markets. Its project-wide scope covers day-ahead markets, balancing requirements and investment incentives for hybrid offshore grids.

My Contribution

  • Develop a public-data-based reconstruction of the Core day-ahead market-clearing process, following key EUPHEMIA market-coupling principles.
  • Integrate publicly available JAO flow-based capacity data and NEMO market bid information into the market model.
  • Apply Core FBMC constraints using published capacity-calculation parameters, including CNECs, PTDFs and RAMs.
  • Reconstruct historical market-clearing outcomes, including electricity prices, and benchmark model outputs against observed market data.
  • Use the modelling framework to support the assessment of alternative offshore bidding-zone configurations and market-integration arrangements.
